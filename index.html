// Our Story · हाम्रो कथा

हाम्रो बारेमाA legacy built on trust.

Founded by Rama Poudel Wagle, our two sister stores on Bazaar Road have been serving the homes, contractors, and electricians of Damauli since 2056 BS — over two and a half decades of reliable service across Tanahun.

Wagle Electricals covers wiring, lighting, switches, fans, motors, chimneys, air conditioners, solar systems, MCBs, GI fittings, and all electrical accessories — stocked with trusted brands for homes and commercial projects alike.

Wagle Generals is our companion store for water and sanitation needs: bathroom fittings, commodes, bathtubs, PPR / CPVC / PVC pipes & fittings, tanks, tools, and everyday hardware supplies.

Honest pricing, genuine brands, and staff who know the trade. Walk in, ask anything — we're here.
